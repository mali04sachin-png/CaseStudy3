Before we check anything

Bharat Freight Solutions is being invited onto Pramaan by Initech Manufacturing Ltd. Nothing gets checked, and no government record gets touched, until you agree. Under the DPDP Act, 2023, here is exactly what that means:

What Pramaan will check, once
  • GST registration status and number, against GSTN
  • PAN, against the Income Tax / Protean database
  • MSME / Udyam classification, against the Udyam registry
  • Bank account and IFSC you provide next — kept as your claim, not auto-verified in this MVP
Who sees the result
  • Only Initech Manufacturing Ltd, the buyer inviting you, sees your verified profile today
  • Any buyer who invites you later sees the same verified profile — you won't re-upload anything
  • No buyer ever sees another buyer's private notes about you, and you never see another vendor's data
What happens to your consent record
  • This consent is timestamped, given a permanent reference ID, and written to an audit log that can't be edited or deleted
  • Pramaan re-checks your records on a schedule afterward — you won't be asked to consent again for routine re-checks
  • You can withdraw consent anytime ("Right to Erase") — your bank details and address are wiped immediately; GST/PAN and the audit trail are kept, anonymized, for tax-audit purposes
  • No fee is ever charged to you — the buyer inviting you covers the verification cost

Scope & open questions

Explicitly not built in this MVP
  • A full ERP or buying platform
  • General cyber-risk scoring
  • Vendor performance tracking (delivery, contract quality)
  • Markets outside India
Open questions the source PRD leaves unresolved
  • No verification-provider API is named — represented generically as "government registry check."
  • The PRD mentions one part of the MVP runs on a "slightly less official channel until upgraded," without saying which — not visualized here.
  • Liability if a verified vendor later commits fraud is flagged as needing legal review, with no answer given — no guarantee language is used anywhere in this prototype as a result.
  • A full provider-outage state (beyond a government-registry retry) isn't described in the source — not designed.
