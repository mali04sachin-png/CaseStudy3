How it works

One check, then a watch that never sleeps.

Pramaan runs the same loop for every vendor, forever — quiet until something that actually matters changes.

  1. Verify once

    The vendor submits GST, PAN and bank details a single time. Pramaan confirms each against the live government registry — what the vendor says is a claim; what the government says is fact.

  2. Watch every day

    The same checks re-run on a schedule. A silent smoke-detector: it only speaks up for changes that affect a real decision, never for trivia.

  3. Alert the right person

    A suspended GST or a new bank account routes straight to Finance or Compliance — with a one-tap action and a permanent, tamper-proof record.
